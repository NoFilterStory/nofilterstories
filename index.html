About Georgiana

The face behind every story.

Georgiana is the soul of No Filters Stories — model, producer, and creative director. She brings vision, authenticity, and fearless energy to every project, transforming locations into stages and moments into art.

Whether draped in silk at ancient ruins or moving freely through open landscapes, Georgiana's presence tells stories that no filter could ever capture. Raw. Real. Unforgettable.
