Meet Georgiana

📍 Velingrad, Bulgaria · Available Worldwide

Hi, I'm Georgiana — born in Romania, raised in Spain, and now based in Velingrad, Bulgaria. Dance brought me in front of the camera. Modelling taught me how it really feels to be there — the good, the uncomfortable, and everything in between.

That experience is what I bring to every shoot.

I hold a Master's in Audiovisual Communication from MasterMedia Valencia. But my real school was festivals, studios, and years of figuring out what makes a person shine on camera.

I specialise in boudoir, dance and vogue-style photography. Movement, light and the right moment turn a simple photo into something you'll never forget.

I speak Spanish, Romanian and English — and I'm working on the Bulgarian.

My style lives between movement and stillness — a fraction of a second where personality, emotion and light come together.

Dramatic shadows, rich colours, and real moments that tell a story without words.

Every session is different because every person is different. But the result is always the same: images that feel alive.

I create photographs that tell your story — no forced poses, no fake smiles. Just you, at your best.

I also take care of everything — props, fabrics, accessories, atmosphere. You show up. I handle the rest.

My goal is simple: every person who steps in front of my lens leaves feeling powerful, beautiful and seen.

Simple. Relaxed. All about you. We start with a quick chat — to understand what you want, what makes you feel good, and what story we want to tell.

During the shoot I guide you every step of the way — movement, angles, expression. No awkward silences. No "just stand there." Just flow.

You leave with images that don't look like photos. They look like you — at your best.

I've been on both sides of the camera. I know how it feels to stand there not knowing what to do — and I know how rare it is to find a photographer who actually guides you through it.

I use dance and movement to help you forget the camera exists. No awkward posing. No uncomfortable silences. Just energy, flow and images that feel like you.

Sessions available in Spanish, Romanian, English and Bulgarian — which means wherever you're from, you'll feel at home in my studio.
