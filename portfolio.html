Our Work

The Art of
Authentic Stories

We don't create images — we create moments. Every session with No Filters Stories is a collaboration between vision, movement, and light. Based in Bulgaria, working internationally with dancers, performers, and high-profile clients who demand something extraordinary.

01

Dance & Movement

Movement is our language. We capture the raw power of dance — the split-second of perfect form, the emotional release, the story told through a single pose. From classical to contemporary, we understand the body in motion.

02

Performer Portraits

Performers live their craft in every frame. We specialise in creating portfolio imagery for dancers, actors, musicians and artists that is both technically perfect and emotionally compelling — images that open doors.

03

Editorial & High-End

For high-profile clients who want something beyond the ordinary. Conceptual editorial work, location shoots across stunning landscapes, and luxury portrait sessions. We travel wherever the story takes us.
